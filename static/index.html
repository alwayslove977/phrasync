Your first transcription downloads an AI model

Choose a Whisper model and select Transcribe. The first run downloads that model to this computer. Phrasync will show the download percentage, transferred size, and estimated time remaining.

It happens once per model.Later transcriptions start directly from the local copy.

Your media stays local.Audio, lyrics, transcripts, projects, and exports are not uploaded to Phrasync.
